# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for Students - Vidyashilp University (17 Aug 2026)


## 1. Course Registration

Eligibility Criteria
Students with pending fees or incomplete documentation
are not eligible for course registration, and attendance will
not be recorded until the registration is fully completed.
• Course Registration on Digii
Course registration will be available on the Digii portal for
eligible students only. Students must select their courses,
verify credits, and submit the registration form online. Students
will not be allowed to attend classes without completing the
course registration.
• Faculty Advisor [Mentor]
Students are advised to consult their Faculty Advisor [Mentor]
to understand and seek clarity on selection of course(s), credits,
etc., prior to course registration.
• Registration Card Submission
After online registration, students must collect the hard copy of
the Registration Card from the Concern Department , get it
signed by the Faculty Advisor [Mentor] and Program Chair ,
and return the signed copy to the Course Registration Card to the
concern department.
• Cohort & Attendance Details
Students can check their cohort details on the Digii portal after
course registration.
Daily attendance status for each course will also be available
on Digii. No attendance will be granted for any missed classes
due to late registration.
• Course Registration Deadline
The maximum permissible period for late registration shall not
be more than one (01) calendar week counted from the specified
date of Registration announced by the University. No extensions
will be granted.
• Late Registration
No late registration shall be permitted. However, considering
medical exigencies, specifically hospitalization, trauma or
contagious disease only, and participation in any
competitions/events a student may be permitted for late
registration with prior approval from the concerned Program
Chair/Dean of concerned School. The maximum period
permissible for late registration due to medical shall not be more
<!-- page:2 -->
than two (02) calendar weeks counted from the specified date of
Registration announced by the University.
• Course Add/Drop
Students may add or drop Courses after consulting the
concerned Faculty Advisor [Mentor] and submit a request for
add or drop to the concerned Program Chair/Dean of Schools
within two weeks of the commencement of the classes as
notified in the Academic Calendar or notifications issued by the
University to this effect.
• Audit Course Registration
A student who desires to register to Audit a Course shall consult
their Faculty Advisor (Mentor) and seek approval of the
concerned Course Instructor. Registration to Audit a Course
shall be permitted after approval by the concerned Program
Chair. The student does not earn credits for Audited Course.

## 2. Student Attendance

Course wise attendance can be check ed on Digii Portal on a
daily basis. Attendance report will be shared with the parents
and students twice per semester by the Office of the Registrar.
• For any concern regarding the attendance, student to contact
the respective faculty members.
• For the End -Term Examination, the attendance recorded on
Digii will be considered as FINAL.
• To appear for end semester examination , the attendance
requirement shall be a minimum of seventy five percent (75%) of
the classes actually conducted in every Course the student has
registered for in the Academic Term.
• Further, (a) if a student / family member suffers serious medical
exigencies of hospitalization, trauma or contagious disease only
(for the student / family member[s] ) the concerned student may
submit the leave request together with the documents to the
Faculty Advisor for recommendation to the Program Chair. The
approved / signed medical applications along with specified
documents to be submitted by the student on the day of
rejoining to the concern department and raise a medical
leave request on Digii portal by uploading all the documents.
Failing which leave on medical grounds will not be accepted.
The medical certificate will be considered only for the entire
day not for specific class/ hours.
• (b) if a student represent s the State / National / International
Events / Competitions, the concerned student may submit the
<!-- page:3 -->
letter to the Director, Campus Life and Faculty Advisor for
recommendation to the Program Chair.
• Such students (a & b) will be given relaxation in attendance
requirement (in the Course(s) where there is a shortage) for
the concerned period of absence . However, the minimum
requirement of attendance will be sixty five percent (65%) of the
classes actually conducted in every Course the student has
registered for in the Academic Term.
• The student shall not be eligible for this special provision if she/he
fails to produce authentic certificates and relevant documents,
including fitness certificate (in the case of Medical), on the day of
rejoining the classes.

## 3. Change of Program

Student may seek transfer from one Program to another
Program before the commencement of the registration for the
Second Year with the due approval of the existing and the new
Program Chair, subject to the eligibility criteria and provisions
of the academic regulations.
• Student to submit request letter signed by parents to the existing
Program Chair and after obtaining the signature, the same letter
to be submitted to the Office of the Registrar for further process.
• The “Credit Transfer Equivalence Committee” shall review the
application of the student for Program change and transfer of
credits. This committee shall submit its recommendations to the
office of the Registrar for approval.
• On no account a transfer from one Program to another will be
allowed beyond the stipulated period.
•

## 4. Summer Term Registration

The Summer Term is an additional Academic Term that may
be offered during the summer break June-July.
• A limited number of Courses may be offered in the Summer
Term. A student cannot demand for a specific Course to be
offered.
• A student may re-register for the Course(s) if offered, by paying
the prescribed fees and completing the course registration process
on or before the last date for Registration as specified in the
Academic Calendar. No late registration shall be permitted.
• The Attendance requirement as per Academic Regulation shall be
applicable to all students registering for Course(s) in the Summer
Term.

## 10. General Guidelines

To ensure a seamless university experience, all students are required to
adhere to the following guidelines:
• Students must wear the ID Card compulsory at all times while on
campus.
• Safety of equipment in the classroom : Utilize upgraded
classroom / lab facilities such as Interactive Display Panels (IDP),
chairs, and tables responsibly. Damages caused by intentional or
negligent behaviour will have consequences.
• Exercise mindfulness during meals : Eat in designated spaces,
especially in the cafeteria. Avoid eating in classrooms / Lab to
maintain a conducive learning environment.
• Use of internet services : University wi -fi is exclusively for
academic use. Using wi -fi other than academic purpose and video
streaming are prohibited. Violations will be viewed seriously.
• Maintain campus cleanliness : Do not litter. CCTV cameras
monitor for compliance and violations will result in consequences.
• Respect shared spaces : Sofas at reception are for guests. Use
alternative seating in the lawn or cafeteria.
• Infirmary usage: Visit the infirmary if you are unwell.
•

## 11. Vehicle and Parking Guidelines

Vehicle access limitations: Vehicles are strictly not permitted to
drive beyond the designated parking point. Exceptions are made
only for emergencies (medical issues or disability).
• Speed limit enforcement : Vehicle speed within the campus is
limited to 20 kmph to ensure safety for all.
• Triple riding prohibition: Triple riding on two-wheelers is
strictly not allowed.
<!-- page:6 -->
• Wearing a helmet is mandatory for two-wheeler riders.
• Reception access: Only vehicles driven by parents / guardians or
authorized drivers may drive till the reception.
• Carpool/bike pool protocol: Vehicles used for student carpooling
or bike pooling must stop at the parking point. Students must walk
from there; vehicles are not permitted to drop off students at
Reception and then park.
• Parking restrictions: Vehicles are not allowed to park at Reception
or in front of the Design Studio. Parking is permitted only in the
designated parking areas.
• Students are advised to regularly check their emails inbox for important University
communication / circular from registrar.office@vidyashilp.edu.in or concern departments.
Timely responses and attention to these communications are essential to avoid missing
important information.
• Your cooperation ensures a safe, respectful, and productive campus environment for all.
